Frequently Asked Questions & 12V Sizing Guide
How long will a 100Ah lithium battery run a 12V camping fridge? +

Under standard ambient temperatures (20°C to 28°C), a typical 40L to 65L single-zone camping fridge (like an Engel, Dometic, or Bushman) draws around 40W to 50W while the compressor is actively running. Because modern fridges cycle on and off (roughly a 35% to 40% duty cycle once down to temperature), the average draw is about 15W to 18W (1.2A to 1.4A at 12.8V).

On a 100Ah LiFePO4 battery (providing roughly 90Ah or ~1,150Wh of usable power), you can expect between 60 and 75 hours of continuous runtime (around 2.5 to 3 full days) without any solar or alternator recharge.

What is the real difference between AGM and LiFePO4 (Lithium) capacity? +

The biggest trap in 12V sizing is assuming a rated 100Ah battery gives you 100Ah of power.

• AGM / Lead-Acid: Should not be discharged beyond 50% Depth of Discharge (DoD) without significantly shortening cell lifespan and causing severe voltage sag under load. A 100Ah AGM only gives you 50Ah of safe usable capacity.
• LiFePO4 Lithium: Can easily be discharged down to 10% remaining capacity (90% DoD) for 3,000+ cycles while maintaining a rock-steady 12.8V to 13.0V output. A 100Ah lithium battery delivers 90Ah of real-world energy—almost double the usable power of an AGM battery half its weight.

How many watts of solar do I need to keep my 12V battery topped up? +

To calculate solar break-even, multiply your continuous hourly load by 24 hours to find your daily energy demand in watt-hours (Wh).

Most sunny regions receive an average of 4.5 Peak Sun Hours per day. After accounting for real-world losses (heat de-rating on solar cells, dust, panel angle, and MPPT controller efficiency—typically around 25% loss), use this rule of thumb:

Required Solar Watts = Daily Wh Demand ÷ (4.5 × 0.75)

For example, running a fridge, canopy camp lights, and phone charging (averaging 30W continuous = 720Wh/day) requires roughly a 200W to 250W solar panel or folding blanket to break even off-grid indefinitely.

How much power does an inverter waste running 240V / 110V gear? +

Inverters convert 12V direct current into mains alternating current, but they are not 100% efficient. Quality pure sine wave inverters operate at roughly 85% to 92% efficiency under normal loads.

Additionally, all inverters draw an idle / tare current simply being turned on (typically 0.8A to 1.5A, or 10W to 20W). If you leave a 2,000W inverter switched on overnight without running any appliances, it will still burn roughly 15Ah to 20Ah of battery capacity just sitting on standby. Whenever possible, run devices directly from native 12V cigarette/Anderson sockets or dedicated USB-C PD outlets.

Can a 100Ah battery run an induction cooktop, kettle, or coffee machine? +

Yes, but with two important conditions:

1. BMS Discharge Rating: A single standard 100Ah lithium battery usually has a 100A continuous Battery Management System (BMS), meaning it can safely deliver a maximum of roughly 1,200W ($12\text{V} \times 100\text{A}$). An 1,800W induction cooktop or kettle requires a 200A BMS (or two 100Ah batteries wired in parallel).
2. Short Bursts Only: High-wattage heating appliances draw huge instantaneous current (~150A), but run for short periods. Boiling a kettle for 3 minutes uses only around 7Ah to 9Ah of capacity.
